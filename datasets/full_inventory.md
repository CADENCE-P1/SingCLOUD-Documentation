## Two findings worth acting on

**1. The platform holds ~849,308 patients.** Every `VW_PATIENT_*_D_Export` table —
gender, race, religion, language, nationality, marital status, residential status —
reports exactly **849,308** rows, with DOB at 849,164 and marital status at 849,012.
One row per patient per attribute makes that the size of the SingCLOUD patient master.
It is the denominator that the "population represented" field on every dataset page
should be stated against, and it is currently absent from all of them.

**2. The ECG example from the rationale is real and specific.** The rationale argues
that a researcher building an AI model needs raw data rather than an interpreted
diagnosis. This platform contains exactly that pair:

| Dataset | Rows | What it holds |
|---|---:|---|
| `VW_CIIMS_ECHO_MEASUREMENTS_D_Export` | 11,520,086 | Quantitative echo **measurements** |
| `VW_CIIMS_ECHO_FINDINGS_D_Export` | 1,130,597 | Echo **findings** — interpretation |

The ten-to-one ratio is the compression of interpretation: many measurements collapse
into one reported finding. A model trained on findings learns to predict cardiologists;
a model trained on measurements learns to predict hearts. Documenting which is which is
the concrete payoff of the standard's §2, and these two tables should be the first
pages written after the governance gap is closed.

## What to do with this

1. **Get the real `data_catalog_summary.txt`.** Everything here becomes verified the
   moment it is replaced by the source file, and the column-level detail in it closes
   the "Not yet profiled" gaps across every existing page.
2. **Decide scope.** Documenting 80 families to the full standard is a substantial
   programme. Documenting the reference tables is nearly free and disproportionately
   useful, since they define the coding standards other datasets depend on.
3. **Reconcile the aliases.** The existing pages use catalogue aliases
   (`Event_Diagnosis_P1`); this inventory uses object filenames
   (`VW_EVENT_DIAGNOSIS_F_Export_31-07-2024_P1.csv`). They are the same data under two
   naming systems, and no mapping between them is written down anywhere.
